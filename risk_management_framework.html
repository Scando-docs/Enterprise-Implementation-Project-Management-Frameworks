Risk Management Framework

Proactive Identification and Mitigation | Standard Implementation Methodology

1. Philosophy & Objective

This framework enforces a proactive, continuous monitoring approach. A risk is an uncertain event that, if it occurs, has a positive or negative effect on project objectives. By actively managing the Risk Register throughout the entire project lifecycle, the Implementation Partner and the Client will minimize surprises and protect the budget, schedule, and quality.

3. Risk Assessment Matrix (Probability × Impact)

4. Risk Response Strategies

🛑 Avoid

Change the project plan to eliminate the threat entirely. Ex: Removing a highly complex, non-critical legacy system integration from the initial deployment scope.

🛡️ Mitigate

Reduce the probability or impact of the risk. Ex: Conducting extra, targeted training sessions to mitigate the risk of low user adoption.

🔄 Transfer

Shift the impact and ownership to a third party. Ex: Relying on the software publisher's cloud SLA for server uptime rather than managing hosting internally.

✅ Accept

Acknowledge the risk but take no proactive action, establishing a Fallback Plan if it occurs. Used primarily for Low Exposure risks.

5. Real-World Implementation Risk Scenarios

Risk Description Exposure Mitigation Strategy (Owner)
Client Master Data is severely corrupted or incomplete during the extraction phase prior to migration. High (6) Mitigate: Initiate data cleansing workshops immediately during the planning phase, weeks before migration formally starts. (Owner: Client Data Stewards)
Resistance to Change: Staff refuse to move away from legacy Excel workflows and resist system adoption. Medium (4) Mitigate: Strong championing by department heads; early system demos to build buy-in and demonstrate efficiency gains. (Owner: Client Project Sponsor)
Scope Creep: Constant requests for custom modules or deviations from the approved Blueprint document. High (6) Avoid: Strictly enforce the Change Management process. Require Steering Committee budget sign-off for any non-standard features. (Owner: Project Manager)
